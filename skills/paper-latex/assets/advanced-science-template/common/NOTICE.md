# Advanced Science drafting resources

The manuscript and supplementary skeletons in this directory are original
Prism writing drafts, distributed under the repository's MIT license.
All numerical entries, graphics and equations are synthetic formatting
examples. The two public demonstration citations exercise bibliography
mechanics only and must be retained only when relevant to the manuscript.

The drafting structure follows the official Advanced Science author guide:
https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/21983844/author-guidelines

The official LaTeX package link identified on that page is:
https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/pb-assets/assets/vch/msp/LaTeX-template-1782154981423.zip

On 2026-11-08, the package returned HTTP 403 through the checked public
routes. Its contents and redistribution license have not been verified.
No file, figure, logo, class or font from that package is mirrored here.
These drafts do not claim to reproduce the official package or final
published layout. Initialization uses repository resources offline.

The separately supplied `prism-wiley-numeric.bst` is a Prism-maintained
BibTeX adaptation, not an official Advanced Science bibliography style.
Its source header preserves the upstream copyright and modification terms
and the LPPL license for Prism modifications. It is derived from Prism's
existing authorized reference adaptation, retains complete article titles
and journal names, lists up to six authors and truncates seven or more to
the first three plus et al., and supports DOI links. The journal accepts
consistent reference formatting at submission; its typesetters prepare the
publication style after acceptance.

Chinese support adds Chinese fonts only. It does not translate the text or
replace the draft's Latin and mathematical typography. Review and
Perspective biographies and portrait slots are optional; their contents
must be supplied by the authors. No research claims, studies or author
achievements are invented by these resources.
