# Journal of Medicinal Chemistry drafting resources

These manuscript and supporting-information skeletons are original Prism
writing drafts under the repository's MIT license. They use the installed
TeX distribution's `article`, `achemso` and BibTeX resources; no publisher
class, illustration, logo or font is redistributed here.

Source checked on 2026-11-08:
https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=jmcmar

The journal does not require a document template. Prism provides a
single-column, double-spaced drafting layout with unnumbered body headings.
Research and Perspective are separate writing skeletons. Perspective is
the journal's interpretive, educational discussion format, rather than an
exhaustive review. It is usually invited; prospective authors should discuss
an unsolicited topic with the editor. Drug Annotation and Viewpoint are
not implemented by these resources.

The abstract prompts suggest approximately 150 words; the author must
check the current guidance for the actual manuscript. Perspective author
biographies belong immediately before the references and should contain
fewer than 125 words each. No qualifications, achievements or research
history are invented in the placeholders.

Prism explicitly selects complete article titles, DOI links, superscript
numeric citations and the achemso default author policy: up to 15 authors
are listed, and a longer list uses the first author plus et al. This is a
draft bibliography configuration, not a claim that achemso package mode
automatically applies a journal-specific class configuration. Authors
remain responsible for accurate references and journal abbreviations.

TOC/abstract graphic source:
https://pubsapp.acs.org/paragonplus/submission/toc_abstract_graphics_guidelines.pdf

The last-page TOC placeholder has an 80 by 42 mm interior, within the
general ACS 82.5 by 44.5 mm area. Its contents must be replaced with the
authors' original artwork, checked at its final readable size. Check the
current journal instructions before submission.

All numerical entries, compound identifiers, diagrams, equations and SAR
tables are synthetic typesetting demonstrations, not research evidence.
The public reference entries demonstrate bibliography mechanics only;
retain them only if relevant to the authors' actual work. No purity,
synthesis, biological activity, ethics approval, literature search or data
availability statement is asserted by these resources. Complete synthesis
procedures for tested compounds belong in the research manuscript's
Experimental section, not solely in supporting information. Any applicable
purity and characterization information must describe the actual work.

The supplement is independently compiled and uses S-numbered pages,
sections, figures, schemes, tables and equations. It makes no assumption
that experiments or a literature search were performed. Chinese support
adds Chinese fonts only; it does not translate text or replace the draft's
Latin or mathematical typography. These are writing and build resources,
not a final submission package or an automatic compliance check.
