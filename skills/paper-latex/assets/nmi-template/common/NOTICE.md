# Nature Machine Intelligence drafting resources

These original Prism manuscript starters are provided under the repository's
MIT license. They are offline writing drafts, not publisher-authored templates
or final submission packages. Figures, numerical values and equations are
synthetic formatting examples, not research results. The sample bibliography
entry is public and demonstrates citation mechanics only.

Consult the journal's current guidance and editors' instructions:
https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/content
https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/initial-formatting
https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/aip-and-formatting

English uses pdfLaTeX and BibTeX; bilingual initialization enables Chinese with
XeLaTeX without translating content. The supplement is independent: build it
with --main supplementary.tex --no-build-script to preserve the main rebuild
entrypoint. Replace all placeholders with accurate author-supplied content.
