# NAR manuscript starters

The three manuscript skeletons, supplementary document and graphical-abstract
slot are original Prism material under the repository's MIT licence. They
illustrate writing structure and contain synthetic examples; they are not
official sample articles or evidence of scientific results.

Prism uses the unmodified OUP authoring class, version 1.5, with its Modern
Large design, as recommended by the [NAR author guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/nar/pages/author-guidelines).
The OUP files retain their own licence and notices, copied during initialization.

`prism-nar.bst` is a separately named, Prism-maintained adaptation of OUP 1.5
`oup-plain.bst`. It retains the original Trickey/Patashnik copyright and
copying terms, which permit changes under a different filename. Prism's
modifications are additionally released under LPPL-1.3c-or-later. The style is
not covered by Prism's MIT licence or presented as an official NAR bibliography
style. Consult its header for the complete attribution and terms.

Replace all sample names, addresses, reserved example URLs, instructions and
synthetic figures or values before submission. Check the journal's current
requirements; this starter does not submit, upload or archive manuscript data.
