# Angew manuscript starters

These four manuscript skeletons and supporting examples are original Prism
material under the repository MIT licence. They contain synthetic examples
and are not official Angewandte Chemie sample manuscripts or scientific results.

The main document uses Prism's renamed adaptation of the Wiley / Overleaf
historical template. Its source, CC BY 4.0 attribution and compatibility changes
are supplied separately during initialization. It is a writing preview and
does not claim to reproduce the journal's final publication layout.

`prism-angew.bst` is separately licensed: it is derived through Prism's NAR
adaptation from the OUP package's `oup-plain.bst`. It retains the original
Trickey/Patashnik copyright and renamed-modification permission. Prism's
modifications use LPPL-1.3c-or-later. Consult the style header; this file is
not covered by the MIT licence and is not an official Angew bibliography style.

Replace sample author details, reserved example email addresses, graphical
slots, synthetic values and instructions with verified material. Review and
Minireview proposals require editorial consideration before a full submission.
Do not infer author biographies, funding, competing interests or research claims.
