# European Journal of Medicinal Chemistry drafting resources

These original Prism research and review skeletons and their supporting
files are distributed under the repository's MIT license. Every numerical
entry, scheme, framed graphic and equation is a synthetic formatting
example. No compound, experiment, literature conclusion or search history
is invented as manuscript content. The two existing public bibliography
entries exercise reference mechanics only; retain them only if relevant.

The journal's official introduction confirms original research and critical
review papers:
https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/european-journal-of-medicinal-chemistry/0223-5234

Current author guidelines:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/european-journal-of-medicinal-chemistry/publish/guide-for-authors

Elsevier LaTeX instructions:
https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/policies-and-guidelines/latex-instructions

On 2026-11-08, the journal-specific author-guide page returned HTTP 403
through the checked public route. Unverified word counts, invitation rules,
keyword limits and submission-file requirements are not asserted here.
The graphical abstract (120 x 48 mm inner drafting frame) and highlights
file are optional writing aids, not a claim of mandatory journal formats.
Authors must check the current instructions before submission.

The installed TeX distribution supplies the unmodified elsarticle class,
elsarticle-num BibTeX style and other required packages. No publisher class,
logo, article, figure, font or package ZIP is mirrored in this directory.
These files support writing and initial manuscript preparation; they do
not claim to reproduce the final published journal layout or prepare a
complete submission package.

Initialization is offline. Chinese support adds Chinese fonts and does not
translate text or replace the class's Latin and mathematical typography.
Supporting material compiles independently. Keep the main rebuild entrypoint
by using --no-build-script for the supplement, graphical abstract and shared
examples. Replace all author/contact placeholders with accurate information.
