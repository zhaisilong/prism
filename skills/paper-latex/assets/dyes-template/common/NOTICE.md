# Dyes and Pigments drafting resources

These original Prism research and review writing skeletons and supporting
files are distributed under the repository's MIT license. Scheme labels,
figures, numbers and equations are synthetic layout demonstrations, not
research results or a record of experiments or literature searches. The
public bibliography entries demonstrate reference mechanics only.

Elsevier's journal scope and review proposal requirement:
https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/dyes-and-pigments/0143-7208
Review proposal form:
https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/dypi-review-paper-proposal.docx
Journal author guidelines:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/dyes-and-pigments/publish/guide-for-authors
Elsevier's general LaTeX instructions:
https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/policies-and-guidelines/latex-instructions
Elsevier's general elsarticle template on Overleaf:
https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/elsevier-article-elsarticle-template/vdzfjgjbckgz

The journal-specific guide returned HTTP 403 when checked on 2026-11-10.
The general Elsevier template is not a journal-specific submission guide.
The journal requests review proposals for editorial approval before review
submission; consult its current instructions and scope before submitting.
Unverified word limits, citation rules and requirements for graphical abstracts
or highlights are not asserted. These files are writing aids and do not
claim to reproduce a journal's final typeset layout.

Initialization is offline. The TeX distribution supplies its unmodified
elsarticle class and elsarticle-num bibliography style; no publisher template
package is copied or downloaded. Bilingual initialization adds Chinese font
support and does not translate the manuscript. Build supplementary material,
graphical abstracts and examples independently with --no-build-script to
preserve the main manuscript rebuild entrypoint.
